{#- The search box, inside its view's GET form (`q`). Enter submits at once; otherwise the form goes two seconds after the typing stops. A submit is a page load, so it waits for a pause rather than chasing every keystroke — and `autofocus` puts the caret back where it was, since the reload it just caused would otherwise drop it. `onsearch` is the clear (✕) button: Chrome and Safari fire `search` when it is pressed, and clearing is a finished thought rather than a pause in one, so it goes at once. Firefox has no `search` event but does fire `input`, so there the ✕ takes the two seconds — which is why the debounce, not the immediate path, is the one that has to work everywhere. -#}
